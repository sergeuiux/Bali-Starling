AWIG AWIG
AWIG AWIG

Awig-awig is Hindu customary law.
It is established by the community, not the government.
By breaking it, you haven’t broken the law. You’ve broken a promise
that everyone made to each other. And that is more serious.

Community decided
to protect the bird

Enforcement doesn’t wait for a ranger.
Everyone in the village is watching.

What happens if you break the law

(01) Break awig-awig
and you pay a fine.
(02) Then you feed the
entire village.
(03) If anyone refuses your
food, you pay them cash.
(04) Then you ask forgiveness
at the temple. Publicly.
